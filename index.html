Features

Everything around your training day, connected.

CoachSync combines planning, nutrition, AI assistance, health sync, and coach-athlete communication so progress is easier to understand and act on.

★

AI Training Assistant

Ask CoachSync AI what to focus on today, refine a planner, generate task ideas, summarize health data, plan the week, and export useful summaries.

☑

Planner and Tasks

Build flexible plans manually or with AI. Break goals into steps, schedule tasks, track completion, and keep daily priorities visible.

🍽

Nutrition and Macros

Log meals, track calories, protein, carbs, fats, hydration, recipes, barcode scans, and AI photo-based meal estimates.

🎯

Sport Goals

Set target weight, daily calories, protein, carbs, fats, and activity-based goals that connect across the app.

♥

Health and Body Composition

Connect health data, review recovery signals, and log InBody-style body composition results over time.

👥

Coach Collaboration

Coaches create public profiles, add training locations, receive athlete requests, manage clients, and message connected athletes.

Everything an athlete needs to stay consistent.

  • Personalized onboarding by sport, training frequency, goals, and role.
  • Daily dashboard for targets, tasks, plans, and focus items.
  • Nutrition logging with macros, hydration, recipes, search, scan, and photo estimation.
  • AI assistant for planning, prioritization, and progress summaries.
  • Health, recovery, and body composition tracking.
  • Messaging with coaches and connected people.

Coach-athlete coordination without scattered tools.

  • Public coach profiles with sports, gym, location, and training places.
  • Collaboration requests from athletes.
  • Client list and athlete profile overview.
  • Shared nutrition and goal visibility where enabled.
  • Notifications and direct messaging.
  • AI-assisted planning and PDF export support.
